# Journal cover thumbnails

Downloaded from Elsevier's official image CDN on 2026-10-25:

- International Journal of Mechanical Sciences: https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/X00207403.jpg

These are publisher-supplied journal display covers. They show representative issues, not necessarily the volume containing the selected paper. The website explicitly labels this distinction. Covers and paper titles both link to the paper's DOI record. No claim is made that the research was featured as cover art.

To replace a cover, add the image here and edit the corresponding record's `cover` field in src/data/publications.json. Preserve the cover's original artwork and aspect ratio.
